Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Lenny Social Media is a personal social-media management and analytics tool. It connects only to accounts that the account owner explicitly authorizes.

Data accessed

The application may access profile information, account statistics, and public content made available through supported platform APIs, according to the permissions granted by the user.

How data is used

Data is used only to provide local analytics, content review, planning, and related account-management functions requested by the authorized user.

Storage

Application credentials, access tokens, and retrieved account data may be stored locally in the user's own project environment. The application does not sell personal data.

Sharing

Data is not sold or shared with advertisers. Data may be processed only by services explicitly used by the authorized user to operate the application.

Revoking access

Users can revoke platform access through the relevant platform's account or app settings. Locally stored data can also be deleted from the user's own system.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: lioneng@hotmail.com
